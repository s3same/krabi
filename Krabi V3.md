# 8-Day Krabi Itinerary - Updated 3-Hotel Version

**Dates:** 8--15 November 2026
**Travellers:** Couple, age 24
**Style:** Relaxing, culturally immersive, trendy, adventurous
**Dietary requirement:** One traveller is gluten-free
**Arrival:** Krabi Airport, 8 Nov at 11:40am
**Departure:** Krabi Airport, 15 Nov at 5:30pm
**Special occasion:** 6-month anniversary on 14 Nov

# Recommended route

**Ao Nang → Koh Lanta → Railay**

With up to three hotels, this version is better suited to the trip you
described. Each base has a distinct purpose:

- **Ao Nang:** convenience, cafés, nightlife and island excursions
- **Koh Lanta:** slow island life, culture, beaches and snorkelling
- **Railay:** dramatic scenery and a romantic anniversary finale

The main improvement is giving Railay its own night rather than treating
it only as a day trip.

# Hotel plan

Dates Base Nights Role

8--10 Nov Ao Nang 3 Adventure + convenience
11--13 Nov Koh Lanta 3 Relaxation + culture
14 Nov Railay 1 Anniversary finale

# Day 1 - Sunday 8 Nov

## Arrival → Ao Nang → sunset

### 11:40am - Arrive at Krabi Airport

Take a pre-booked transfer/taxi directly to Ao Nang.

### 1:00pm - Arrive and drop bags

Check into your Ao Nang hotel.

### 2:00pm - Lunch + café

Try **Shatterbox Kitchen & Bar** or another modern Ao Nang café.

### 4:30pm - Ao Nang Beach

Take a relaxed beachfront walk.

### 6:00pm - Sunset drinks

Find a beachfront bar or café and ease into the holiday.

### 7:30pm - Thai dinner

Try **Jungle Kitchen** for a more local-feeling meal rather than eating
directly on the busiest tourist strip.

### Stay

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**

Alternative: **COSI Krabi Ao Nang Beach**

# Day 2 - Monday 9 Nov

## Ao Nang → Krabi Town + night markets

Keep this day focused on local atmosphere, food and an easy change of pace
after arrival day.

### 9:00am - Slow breakfast in Ao Nang

Have breakfast at your hotel or a nearby café, then head toward Krabi Town.

### 10:30am - Krabi Town

Explore the town centre at a relaxed pace and take in the riverside, local
shops and everyday southern-Thai atmosphere.

### Lunch

Choose a local restaurant or café in town. With the gluten-free requirement,
confirm sauces, marinades and preparation methods before ordering.

### Afternoon - Riverside + local sights

Continue exploring Krabi Town, with time for coffee and photos rather than
trying to pack in too many attractions.

### 5:00pm onward - Night markets

Spend the evening around Krabi Town's night-market areas, browsing street
food, snacks, crafts and local stalls.

For gluten-free eating, ask vendors about sauces and shared cooking surfaces
and choose simple dishes where the ingredients and preparation are clear.

### 8:30--9:00pm - Return to Ao Nang

Head back to the hotel for a relaxed evening before the early start tomorrow.

# Day 3 - Tuesday 10 Nov

## Ao Nang 🐠 Hong Islands + snorkelling

This is your first major tropical day.

### 7:00--7:30am - Depart Ao Nang

Book a **small-group Hong Islands tour** rather than a large party boat.

Look for an itinerary including:

- Hong Island lagoon
- Snorkelling
- Swimming
- Lading/Pakbia area
- Beach time
- Lunch

### What to request

> Small group, early departure, snorkelling, Hong Lagoon, no party boat.

### 2:30--3:00pm - Return to Ao Nang

Shower, nap and relax.

### 4:30pm - Sunset

If you have the energy, head to **Khaothong Hill** for elevated views
over the limestone islands.

Otherwise, keep it easy at Ao Nang.

### Dinner

Try a contemporary restaurant such as **MESA Restaurant & Bar**, or
choose another gluten-free-friendly option after checking with the
restaurant.

# Day 4 - Wednesday 11 Nov

## Ao Thalane kayaking → Koh Lanta

This is your adventure-without-being-extreme day.

### 7:30am - Ao Thalane kayaking

Book a guided kayaking experience.

Expect:

- Mangrove channels
- Limestone cliffs
- Jungle
- Wildlife
- Calm waterways

### 1:00pm - Lunch + collect luggage

### 2:00pm-ish - Transfer to Koh Lanta

Use a shared/private minivan as the reliable option.

Early-November ferry schedules can be seasonal, so don't build this day
around a ferry unless the operator has confirmed the exact 11 November
service.

### 5:00pm - Check in

Stay around **Long Beach or Klong Dao**.

### 5:30pm - Beach sunset

### 7:30pm - Dinner

Keep the evening relaxed.

### Stay

**Long Beach Chalet**

Alternative: **Coco Lanta Resort**

# Day 5 - Thursday 12 Nov

## Koh Lanta 4 Islands + Emerald Cave

Book a **Koh Lanta 4 Islands / Emerald Cave** tour.

Potential stops include:

- Koh Mook
- Koh Kradan
- Koh Ngai
- Koh Cheuak
- Emerald Cave

### Activities

- Snorkelling
- Swimming
- Beaches
- Boat rides
- Emerald Cave exploration

This is adventurous without requiring diving, rock climbing or technical
hiking.

### Important

Bring a waterproof phone pouch for the Emerald Cave swim.

### 5:00pm - Return to resort

Do nothing.

Shower, nap, sunset and dinner.

### Gluten-free

Tell the tour operator about the gluten-free requirement **before
booking**, particularly because lunch is often included.

### Dinner

Try a gluten-free-friendly option such as **Cozy Restaurant**, after
confirming preparation practices.

# Day 6 - Friday 13 Nov

## Koh Lanta cultural + slow day

Make this your most relaxed day.

### Morning - Sleep in

Breakfast, pool and beach.

### 11:30am - Lanta Old Town

Explore the traditional wooden shophouses, waterfront and pier.

The area is especially interesting for cultural immersion because Koh
Lanta has Thai-Chinese, Thai-Muslim and Sea Gypsy heritage.

### Lunch / coffee

Try a waterfront café.

**Rareview** is a good option for an over-water setting and photos.

### Afternoon - Tung Yee Peng

Visit **Tung Yee Peng** and consider a community-based mangrove
experience.

Look for experiences involving:

- Mangroves
- Community tourism
- Local livelihoods
- Conservation
- Longtail boats

### Late afternoon - Kantiang Bay

Beach, swimming and sunset.

### Dinner

Try **Yang Garden Restaurant** for a more stylish/fusion dinner.

Keep this dinner nice but don't make it the special occasion meal.

# Day 7 - Saturday 14 Nov

# 💕 6-month anniversary

## Full Railay day + anniversary dinner + The Grotto

Make Railay the centrepiece of the anniversary. Since this is now a full
Railay day, aim to arrive from Koh Lanta early enough to enjoy the beach,
scenery and relaxed pace before dinner.

### Early morning - Koh Lanta → Railay

Leave Koh Lanta after an early breakfast.

Use a boat only if the exact seasonal service is confirmed for 14
November. Otherwise, use a minivan + longtail combination.

### Late morning - Check in and settle into Railay

Drop your bags and start exploring without rushing.

### Morning / early afternoon - Full Railay exploration

Explore:

- Railay West
- Phra Nang Beach
- Princess Cave
- Railay's beachfront paths
- Limestone-cliff viewpoints that do not require technical scrambling

Swim, relax and take photos throughout the afternoon.

**Skip the Railay viewpoint/lagoon scramble** because it is a slippery,
rope-assisted climb and doesn't fit your preference for avoiding extreme
activities.

### 3:30--4:30pm - Slow down + get ready

Return to the hotel, shower and dress up for your anniversary dinner.

### 4:30pm - Golden-hour couple photos

Take dressed-up photos around Railay and Phra Nang before dinner.

# Anniversary dinner

## The Grotto at Rayavadee

**Recommended dinner:** The Grotto at Rayavadee

This is the strongest fit for your anniversary if you are staying in
Railay.

The restaurant is set beneath a limestone cliff beside Phra Nang Beach,
giving you a dramatically romantic setting.

### Suggested timing

**4:30pm** - Get ready
**5:15pm** - Couple photos around Railay / Phra Nang
**5:45pm** - Head toward The Grotto
**6:00pm** - Twilight dinner
**7:00--8:30pm** - Dinner + drinks
**8:30pm onward** - Return through Railay

### Gluten-free requirement

Contact Rayavadee in advance.

Use clear wording:

> "One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which
> dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be
> avoided."

Don't rely on explaining this only when you arrive.

### Stay

Choose a Railay hotel/resort for this night.

The point of this night is to make the anniversary feel like a
destination within the destination.

# 🌾 Gluten-free game plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but gluten can enter
through:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Sauces
- Battered foods
- Fried foods
- Shared fryers
- Cross-contamination

For a strict requirement, explain the situation clearly rather than
simply saying "no bread."

Useful Thai phrase:

> **ฉันแพ้กลูเตน**
> _Chan pae gluten_
> "I am allergic/intolerant to gluten."

For a strict requirement:

> **อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน**
> "The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food
> containing gluten."

For the anniversary dinner, contact the restaurant ahead of time and
discuss the requirement before arrival.

# Why this version is better for you

The three-hotel version gives you three distinct experiences without
excessive packing:

### Ao Nang

**Fun + convenience + excursions**

### Koh Lanta

**Slow island life + culture + snorkelling**

### Railay

**Romantic scenery + anniversary**

You get:

- 3 hotel bases
- 2 major island-hopping days
- 1 kayaking adventure
- 1 full Railay day
- 1 dedicated Krabi Town / night-market day
- 1 dedicated Koh Lanta cultural day
- Multiple sunset/photo locations
- Plenty of downtime
- A memorable anniversary night
- No rock climbing or scuba diving
- Gluten-free considerations built into the food planning

The main logistical caveat is the **seasonal transition for boat/ferry
services around early November**. Use minivan transfers as the reliable
default and only rely on ferries/boats between islands once the operator
confirms the exact service for your date.
