## Recommended Route

**Singapore → Ao Nang → Ko Lanta → Mahutsachan Khlong Thom → Krabi Airport**

All hotels are booked. The main transport choice to make is **Thursday 12 November**:

- **Ferry / speedboat + local taxi (your current preference):** travel directly from Ao Nang to Saladan Pier, then take a local taxi to Lanta Sand Resort & Spa. This is the best fit if you are happy to skip Klong Root kayaking on 12 November.
- **Klong Root + private car:** keep the freshwater kayaking stop, then have a pre-booked car continue directly from Klong Root to Lanta Sand Resort & Spa via the vehicle ferry. This is the practical option if kayaking is a priority.
- **Shared minivan + vehicle ferry:** the cheaper, road-based alternative from Ao Nang to the resort; it is not compatible with a Klong Root stop unless a driver separately agrees to collect you there.

The hotel sequence is **Carlton City Hotel Singapore (7 Nov) → Aspira Escape Ao Nang Krabi (8–11 Nov) → Lanta Sand Resort & Spa (12–13 Nov) → Mahutsachan Khlong Thom (14 Nov)**. Leave the final evening at Mahutsachan for rest and wellness, then pre-book the airport transfer for the morning of 15 November.

### Anniversary Dinner — Katara Bar & Restaurant Krabi

**Recommended booking: Sunday 8 November at 5:00pm.** Ao Nang sunset is around **6:03pm** on this date, so a 5:00pm table gives you time to order drinks and starters, begin dinner before sunset, and enjoy the changing light while eating. Ask for an **ocean-facing table** and mention that it is your six-month anniversary.

- **Best all-round choice:** reserve **5:00pm** and aim to order your first dishes by 5:20–5:30pm.
- **If you prefer a leisurely pre-dinner drink:** ask whether a **4:45pm arrival / 5:00pm table** can be arranged.
- **If the restaurant confirms that the kitchen or dinner service is busiest at sunset:** reserve **5:15pm**, but this leaves less time to settle in before the sun reaches the horizon.

Katara's official site lists the venue as beachfront, opens from **4:00pm on Sundays**, and says reservations are highly recommended; walk-ins are subject to availability. Make the reservation directly via [Katara's official website](https://www.katarabeachclubkrabi.com/) or call **+66 62 526 2767** / email **manager@katarabeachclubs.com**. Reconfirm opening hours and the table location shortly before the trip.

**Gluten-free request:** contact Katara before paying a deposit or confirming the booking. Explain that one diner needs strict gluten-free food, ask about sauces / marinades and shared preparation surfaces, and request that the booking note be added to the reservation.

Reference: [Ao Nang sunset times for November 2026](https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/%401120400?month=11) · [Katara beachfront dining and reservations](https://www.katarabeachclubkrabi.com/katara-beachfront-dining)

# Day 3 – Tuesday 10 Nov

## 🏖️ Ao Nang beach-club day - full sun, day bed + slow afternoon

**Confirmed plan:** Make 10 Nov a deliberately low-effort recovery day after the 9 Nov boat tour.

The main objective is exactly what you described: **book a day bed / lounger, stay in the sun, swim when you feel like it, eat lunch on-site and do very little else.**

### Beach-club recommendations

#### 1. THE BEACH Club Krabi - Klong Muang

This is the clearest fit for the day-bed brief because its current booking page explicitly offers **lawn sun loungers, cushion loungers, sea-view cabanas and pool bean bags**. Current published pricing is:

- **Lawn sun lounger:** THB 1,000/person, including THB 500 food-and-drink credit
- **Pool bean bag:** THB 1,000/person, including THB 500 food-and-drink credit
- **Cushion lounger:** THB 1,500/person, including THB 750 food-and-drink credit
- **Sea-view cabana:** THB 4,500 for up to 3 people, including THB 2,000 food-and-drink credit
- Open **10:00am–10:00pm**

For the two of you, the **lawn sun loungers** match the brief most closely if you want a proper sunny day-bed setup. The **sea-view cabana** makes more sense when shade, privacy and lounging space matter more than keeping the cost down.

The club's current FAQ also says guests can arrive with suitcases and that the team can store luggage. Showers and locker rooms are available.

**Book:** [THE BEACH Club Krabi](https://thebeachkrabi.com/beach-club/)
**Luggage / FAQ:** [THE BEACH Club Krabi – Contact & FAQ](https://thebeachkrabi.com/contact/)

# Gluten-Free Game Plan

Tell every operator about the gluten-free requirement **before booking**, especially where lunch is included.

Contact restaurants ahead of time, especially for the **Day 1 anniversary dinner**.
