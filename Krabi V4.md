# 8-Day Krabi Itinerary - Updated 3-Hotel Version

**Dates:** 8--15 November 2026
**Travellers:** Couple, age 24
**Style:** Relaxing, culturally immersive, trendy, adventurous
**Dietary requirement:** One traveller is gluten-free
**Arrival:** Krabi Airport, 8 Nov at 11:40am
**Departure:** Krabi Airport, 15 Nov at 5:30pm
**Special occasion:** 6-month anniversary dinner on 9 Nov

# Recommended route

**Ao Nang → Koh Lanta → Railay**

With up to three hotels, this version is better suited to the trip you
described. Each base has a distinct purpose:

- **Ao Nang:** convenience, cafés, nightlife and island excursions
- **Koh Lanta:** slow island life, culture, beaches and snorkelling
- **Railay:** dramatic scenery and a romantic anniversary finale

The main improvement is giving Railay its own night rather than treating
it only as a day trip.

# Hotel plan

Dates Base Nights Role

8--10 Nov Ao Nang 3 Adventure + convenience
11--13 Nov Koh Lanta 3 Relaxation + culture
14 Nov Railay 1 Anniversary finale

# Day 1 - Sunday 8 Nov

## Arrival → Ao Nang → sunset

### 11:40am - Arrive at Krabi Airport

Take a pre-booked transfer/taxi directly to Ao Nang.

### 1:00pm - Arrive and drop bags

Check into your Ao Nang hotel.

### 2:00pm - Lunch + café

Try **Shatterbox Kitchen & Bar** or another modern Ao Nang café.

### 4:30pm - Ao Nang Beach

Take a relaxed beachfront walk.

### 6:00pm - Sunset drinks

Find a beachfront bar or café and ease into the holiday.

### 7:30pm - Thai dinner

Try **Jungle Kitchen** for a more local-feeling meal rather than eating
directly on the busiest tourist strip.

### Stay

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**

Alternative: **COSI Krabi Ao Nang Beach**

# Day 2 - Monday 9 Nov

## 💕 6-month anniversary

## Krabi Town + anniversary sunset dinner

Keep the daytime local and relaxed, then return to Ao Nang early enough to
dress up and enjoy a proper sunset dinner for the anniversary.

### 9:00am - Slow breakfast in Ao Nang

Have breakfast at your hotel or a nearby café, then head toward Krabi Town.

### 10:30am - Krabi Town

Explore the town centre at a relaxed pace and take in the riverside, local
shops and everyday southern-Thai atmosphere.

### Lunch

Choose a local restaurant or café in town. With the gluten-free requirement,
confirm sauces, marinades and preparation methods before ordering.

### Afternoon - Riverside + local sights

Continue exploring Krabi Town, but keep the afternoon flexible rather than
staying too late. The priority is getting back to Ao Nang with time to shower,
dress up and reach the coast before golden hour.

### 3:30--4:00pm - Return to Ao Nang

Head back to the hotel, freshen up and get ready for the anniversary dinner.

### 5:00--5:30pm - Arrive for sunset

Aim to be seated before sunset. Current dining guidance for Ao Nang puts sunset
around 6:15--6:45pm, with beachfront sunset tables often worth reserving ahead.

### 5:30--7:30pm - Anniversary dinner

## Anniversary sunset dinner

Your two strongest matches from the places you already have in mind are:

### REEVE Beach Club Krabi

A polished beachfront option on Ao Nang Beach with all-day dining, cocktails,
live music and daily fire-dance entertainment. REEVE specifically markets its
sunset setting for romantic evenings.

**Best for:** a lively, stylish beach-club atmosphere with dinner, drinks and
entertainment.

**Plan:** reserve a table close to the beachfront and ask for a sunset-facing
position. For the anniversary, arrive early enough to enjoy the light before
the evening entertainment starts.

### Katara Bar & Restaurant / Katara Beach Club Krabi

A more design-led beachfront dining experience with its "Beach Theater"
concept, modern Thai flavours, drinks and broad Andaman views. Current
operating information lists evening service from around 4--5pm onward.

**Best for:** a more contemporary, couple-friendly setting where the view is
the main event.

**Plan:** reserve in advance and request one of the seats/tables with the
clearest horizon view.

### Other sunset / beach-club-style options

**The Beach Seafood & Grill** - Directly on the sands of Ao Nang Beach, with
seafood, grills, cocktails and an explicitly sunset-focused beachfront setup.
A good alternative when you want a classic beach dinner without needing a
clifftop restaurant.

**The Last Fisherman Bar** - A more casual, toes-in-the-sand beach-bar option
on Ao Nang Beach, with seafood, Thai/Asian food, barbecue, cocktails and a
front-row sunset setting. Better for a relaxed, barefoot-feeling evening than
a dressed-up fine-dining mood.

**C2 Beach Club** - A quieter beachfront bar toward the southern end of Ao
Nang, with seating on the sand and a more laid-back atmosphere. Better suited
to drinks and a casual meal than a formal anniversary dinner.

**Lae Lay Grill** - A hillside seafood restaurant rather than a beach club,
but it gives you a wide elevated view over Ao Nang and the islands at sunset.
This is the alternative to choose when the priority is the panorama rather
than being on the sand.

### My anniversary setup

For the mood you described, build the evening around **5:00--5:30pm arrival,
sunset around 6:15--6:45pm, dinner through 7:30pm**, then a relaxed drink or
short beach walk before returning to the hotel.

The main practical choice is between:

- **REEVE:** more energetic beach-club feel, with live entertainment/fire show
- **Katara:** more design-led and dining-focused, with the sea as the backdrop
- **The Beach Seafood & Grill:** classic beachfront dinner + sunset
- **The Last Fisherman:** most casual / toes-in-the-sand feel
- **Lae Lay Grill:** best for a higher, panoramic sunset setting

Do not treat the bullets above as a ranking; they are different styles for the
same sunset-dinner brief.

### Gluten-free requirement

Contact the restaurant before booking.

Use clear wording:

> "One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which
> dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be
> avoided."

For a strict requirement, confirm sauces, marinades, batter, frying oil and
shared preparation surfaces before ordering.

### 8:00pm onward - Easy anniversary night

Return to the hotel without adding another major activity. The next morning is
your early Hong Islands day.

# Day 7 - Saturday 14 Nov

## Full Railay day + sunset + dinner suggestions

Railay is now your relaxed post-anniversary finale rather than the anniversary

### Early morning - Koh Lanta → Railay

Leave Koh Lanta after an early breakfast.

Use a boat only if the exact seasonal service is confirmed for 14 November.

### Late morning - Check in and settle into Railay

Drop your bags and start exploring without rushing.

### Morning / early afternoon - Full Railay exploration

Explore:

- Railay West
- Phra Nang Beach
- Princess Cave
- Railay's beachfront paths
- Limestone-cliff viewpoints that do not require technical scrambling

Swim, relax and take photos throughout the afternoon.

**Skip the Railay viewpoint/lagoon scramble** because it is a slippery,

### 3:30--5:30pm - Slow afternoon + sunset

Head back toward Railay West for the late afternoon. This is the right window

Railay West is the side of the peninsula to prioritise for sunset.

### 6:00--8:00pm - Dinner in Railay

## Railay dinner suggestions

Because the anniversary dinner is now on 9 Nov, you can choose a more casual
or local-feeling dinner in Railay and let the scenery do the work.

### Sunset / beachfront

**Krua Village Restaurant at Railay Village Resort** - A beachfront
restaurant on Railay West with Thai and Western food, fresh seafood and a
dedicated "Sunset Beach Dinner" setup. Good choice for a peaceful meal while
staying close to the sand.

**The Sunset Restaurant at Sand Sea Resort** - Directly on Railay West.
The resort says its restaurant offers fresh seafood, all-day dining and a
special sunset dinner setup that can be reserved in advance.

### Thai / local

**Railay Family Restaurant** - Casual, central and seafood/Thai-focused.
Good for a straightforward dinner around Railay Walking Street.

**Mangrove Restaurant** - A long-established casual choice with Thai and
seafood dishes; current traveller reports continue to mention grilled chicken,
fish and other Thai staples.

**Welcome Back Restaurant** - Another casual Thai/seafood option on the
walking-street side, useful when you want something simple rather than a resort
dinner.

### Indian / gluten-free options

**Kohinoor Indian Restaurant & Pizza** - Indian and Italian menu with current
listing information showing gluten-free options. This is useful as a backup
when the gluten-free requirement makes you want a broader menu and clearer
non-Thai choices.

### Drinks / atmosphere

**The Last Bar** - On Railay East, with outdoor seating, a full bar and live
entertainment. It is more of a drinks-and-atmosphere stop than a sunset
restaurant, so it works well after dinner rather than as your main sunset meal.

### Suggested Railay evening

**5:30pm** - Sunset walk / beach photos
**6:15pm** - Dinner
**7:45pm** - Walk Railay Walking Street
**8:30pm onward** - Drinks at The Last Bar or back to the hotel

Keep this night flexible rather than booking a complicated multi-stop plan.

### Gluten-free

Use the same strict gluten-free approach as earlier in the trip. Confirm
sauces, marinades, batter, frying oil and cross-contamination practices before
ordering. For Kohinoor, current listing information specifically notes
gluten-free options, but you should still confirm preparation with the
restaurant.

### Stay

Choose a Railay hotel/resort for this night.

The point of this night is now to enjoy Railay itself after the anniversary,
rather than to repeat the special-occasion dinner format.

# 🌾 Gluten-free game plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but gluten can enter
through:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Sauces
- Battered foods
- Fried foods
- Shared fryers
- Cross-contamination

For a strict requirement, explain the situation clearly rather than
simply saying "no bread."

Useful Thai phrase:

> **ฉันแพ้กลูเตน**
> _Chan pae gluten_
> "I am allergic/intolerant to gluten."

For a strict requirement:

> **อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน**
> "The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food
> containing gluten."

For the anniversary dinner, contact the restaurant ahead of time and
discuss the requirement before arrival.
