### Gluten-free note

Tell the tour operator about the gluten-free requirement when booking, especially because lunch is included.

Suggested wording:

> “One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be avoided.”

For dinner:

> “One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely or if you have dedicated fryers/surfaces.”

Useful Thai phrases:

- **ฉันแพ้กลูเตน (Chan pae gluten)** – “I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”
- **อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน** – “The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food containing gluten.”

# Day 4 – Wednesday 11 Nov

## 🏖️ Ao Nang beach-club day — full sun, day bed + slow afternoon

**Confirmed plan:** Make 11 Nov a deliberately low-effort recovery day after two consecutive boat-tour days.

The main objective is exactly what you described: **book a day bed / lounger, stay in the sun, swim when you feel like it, eat lunch on-site and do very little else.**

### Beach-club recommendations

#### 1. THE BEACH Club Krabi — Klong Muang

This is the clearest fit for the day-bed brief because its current booking page explicitly offers **lawn sun loungers, cushion loungers, sea-view cabanas and pool bean bags**. Current published pricing is:

- **Lawn sun lounger:** THB 1,000/person, including THB 500 food-and-drink credit
- **Pool bean bag:** THB 1,000/person, including THB 500 food-and-drink credit
- **Cushion lounger:** THB 1,500/person, including THB 750 food-and-drink credit
- **Sea-view cabana:** THB 4,500 for up to 3 people, including THB 2,000 food-and-drink credit
- Open **10:00am–10:00pm**

For the two of you, the **lawn sun loungers** match the brief most closely if you want a proper sunny day-bed setup. The **sea-view cabana** makes more sense when shade, privacy and lounging space matter more than keeping the cost down.

A major practical advantage: the club's current FAQ explicitly says guests can arrive with suitcases and that the team can **safely store luggage**. It also says showers and locker rooms are available. That makes this the strongest option to keep in reserve for any day where luggage is involved.

**Book:** [THE BEACH Club Krabi](https://thebeachkrabi.com/beach-club/)
**Luggage / FAQ:** [THE BEACH Club Krabi – Contact & FAQ](https://thebeachkrabi.com/contact/)

#### 2. C2 Beach Club Ao Nang — beachfront day bed, minimal travel

C2 is the most straightforward option if you do not want to leave Ao Nang. Recent traveller reports describe **two-person day beds with parasols** and an all-day lounging setup, with food and drinks available on-site. Current published day-bed pricing is not clearly listed on the club's own site, so treat any quoted price you see online as something to reconfirm directly.

This is a good choice when the priority is **simple: walk / short transfer to the beach, claim a bed, read, eat, swim, repeat**.

**Reference:** [C2 Beach Club Ao Nang – current Tripadvisor listing](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1507054-d19835203-Reviews-C2_Beach_Club_Aonang_Krabi-Ao_Nang_Krabi_Province.html)

#### 3. REEVE Beach Club — stylish Ao Nang option + stay into sunset

REEVE is directly on Ao Nang Beach and currently publishes **08:00–24:00** opening hours. Its own site positions it as a beachfront dining / beach-club venue, and recent 2026 Ao Nang discussions specifically mention sunbeds / beanbags near the sand.

Choose this if you want the day to gradually become a sunset / drinks evening rather than heading back to the hotel early.

**Important date check:** current listings show a temporary closure through **30 Oct 2026**, which is before your 11 Nov visit. Reconfirm the club's reopening status close to the trip.

**Book / check:** [REEVE Beach Club](https://www.reevebeachclub.com/)

# Gluten-Free Game Plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but watch out for **soy sauce, marinades, battered foods and shared fryers**.

## Useful Thai phrase

**ฉันแพ้กลูเตน (Chan pae gluten)** – “I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”

## Strict requirement

**อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน**

## Booking rule for tours

Tell every operator about the gluten-free requirement **before booking**, especially where lunch is included.

## Restaurant rule

Contact restaurants ahead of time, especially for the **Day 1 anniversary dinner**.
