### Tour highlights

- Snorkel among colorful marine life and vibrant coral reefs at **Chicken Island**
- Walk along the unique sandbar at **Koh Tup** and take photos
- Sunbathe at **Koh Poda** and enjoy a beachside lunch with typical Thai flavours
- Marvel at the limestone cliffs and **Phra Nang Cave Beach** in the Railay area
- Choose between a private or shared tour and benefit from hotel transfers, depending on the selected operator/package

### Full description / booking notes

The supplied tour description is a premium longtail-boat experience visiting four famous Krabi islands, with a shared or private option, lunch, fresh fruit, water and snorkelling equipment.

Typical sequence:

1. **Chicken Island** – snorkelling around the island's distinctive rock formation and coral reef.
2. **Koh Tup / Thale Waek** – sandbar experience connecting Koh Tup and Koh Mor during low tide.
3. **Koh Poda** – lunch on the beach, swimming, sunbathing and snorkelling.
4. **Phra Nang Cave Beach** – limestone cliffs, clear water and the famous cave in the Railay area.

Private tours may offer early-morning or sunset options depending on the operator/package.

### Lunch

**Included with the selected tour/package.** Confirm the gluten-free meal before booking.

### Dinner

### Nightlife

After dinner, make **Ao Nang Landmark Night Market** the easy social stop: browse the stalls, grab fruit or a small dessert, and keep the night moving at a relaxed pace. Current listings show the market operating into the evening. For a drink afterward, choose one nearby beach bar rather than another full itinerary block.

The aim is **food + market + one drink**, not a late night after the boat tour.

### Hotel

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**

### Gluten-free note

Tell the tour operator about the gluten-free requirement when booking, especially because lunch is included.

Suggested wording:

> "One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be avoided."

For dinner:

> "One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely or if you have dedicated fryers/surfaces."

Useful Thai phrases:

- **ฉันแพ้กลูเตน (Chan pae gluten)** – "I am allergic/intolerant to gluten."
- **อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน** – "The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food containing gluten."

### Beach-club recommendations

#### 1. THE BEACH Club Krabi - Klong Muang

This is the clearest fit for the day-bed brief because its current booking page explicitly offers **lawn sun loungers, cushion loungers, sea-view cabanas and pool bean bags**. Current published pricing is:

- **Lawn sun lounger:** THB 1,000/person, including THB 500 food-and-drink credit
- **Pool bean bag:** THB 1,000/person, including THB 500 food-and-drink credit
- **Cushion lounger:** THB 1,500/person, including THB 750 food-and-drink credit
- **Sea-view cabana:** THB 4,500 for up to 3 people, including THB 2,000 food-and-drink credit
- Open **10:00am–10:00pm**

For the two of you, the **lawn sun loungers** match the brief most closely if you want a proper sunny day-bed setup. The **sea-view cabana** makes more sense when shade, privacy and lounging space matter more than keeping the cost down.

The club's current FAQ also says guests can arrive with suitcases and that the team can store luggage. Showers and locker rooms are available.

**Book:** [THE BEACH Club Krabi](https://thebeachkrabi.com/beach-club/)

#### 2. C2 Beach Club Ao Nang - beachfront day bed, minimal travel

C2 is the straightforward option if you do not want to leave Ao Nang. Recent traveller reports describe **two-person day beds with parasols** and an all-day lounging setup, with food and drinks available on-site. Current published day-bed pricing is not clearly listed on the club's own site, so treat any quoted price you see online as something to reconfirm directly.

**Reference:** [C2 Beach Club Ao Nang – current Tripadvisor listing](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1507054-d19835203-Reviews-C2_Beach_Club_Aonang_Krabi-Ao_Nang_Krabi_Province.html)

#### 3. REEVE Beach Club - stylish Ao Nang option + stay into sunset

REEVE is directly on Ao Nang Beach and currently publishes **08:00–24:00** opening hours. Its own site positions it as a beachfront dining / beach-club venue, and recent 2026 Ao Nang discussions mention sunbeds / beanbags near the sand.

Choose this if you want the day to flow naturally into sunset drinks.

**Important date check:** current listings show a temporary closure through **30 Oct 2026**, which is before your 10 Nov visit. Reconfirm the club's reopening status close to the trip.

**Book / check:** [REEVE Beach Club](https://www.reevebeachclub.com/)
