# 8-Day Krabi Itinerary - Updated Proposed Itinerary (1 Night Railay / 3 Nights Lanta)

Dates: 8–15 November 2026
Travellers: Couple, age 24
Style: Relaxing, culturally immersive, trendy, adventurous
Dietary requirement: One traveller is gluten-free
Arrival: Krabi Airport, 8 Nov at 11:40am
Departure: Krabi Airport, 15 Nov at 5:30pm
Special occasion: 6-month anniversary dinner on 9 Nov

---

## Recommended route

Ao Nang → Railay → Koh Lanta

This updated version keeps three distinct experiences:

- Ao Nang: convenience, cafés, nightlife, island tours, anniversary dinner
- Railay: dramatic limestone scenery, beaches and a short romantic stay
- Koh Lanta: slow island life, culture, beaches, snorkelling and the final wind-down

The main updates in this version are:

- Monday 9 November is now an island tour day instead of a Krabi Town day.
- Tuesday 10 November’s Hong Islands tour has more detail on what to expect.
- A detailed Phi Phi Islands vs Krabi 4 Islands comparison has been added to help you decide which tour to book for Monday 9 November.
- Railay → Koh Lanta is now planned as Railay → Ao Nang by boat, then Grab / taxi / private transfer to Koh Lanta.

---

## Key changes in this updated version

### 1. Monday 9 November now includes an island tour

Instead of spending the daytime in Krabi Town, Day 2 is now built around an island tour.

You still keep the 6-month anniversary dinner on 9 November, but the timing of dinner may change depending on which island tour you choose.

Your two main options are:

- Phi Phi Islands full-day tour
- Krabi 4 Islands half-day / afternoon tour

A detailed decision guide is included later in this file.

### 2. Tuesday 10 November Hong Islands tour has been expanded

More information has been added on:

- Expected timing
- Likely stops
- Snorkelling and beach time
- Lunch
- Fitness level required
- What to bring
- Gluten-free considerations
- What to ask when booking

### 3. Railay → Koh Lanta transfer updated

Your preferred transfer is now:

Railay → Ao Nang by longtail boat → Grab / taxi / private car to Koh Lanta

This avoids relying on uncertain direct boat services between Railay and Koh Lanta.

## Gluten-free note for the anniversary island tour and dinner

For the tour:

Tell the operator about the gluten-free requirement when booking, especially if lunch is included.

Useful wording:

“One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be avoided.”

For the anniversary dinner:

Contact the restaurant before booking.

Use clear wording:

“One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be avoided.”

For a strict requirement, confirm:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Sauces
- Batter
- Frying oil
- Shared fryers
- Shared preparation surfaces

Useful Thai phrase:

ฉันแพ้กลูเตน
Chan pae gluten
“I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”

For a strict requirement:

อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน
“The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food containing gluten.”

# Decision guide: Phi Phi Islands tour vs Krabi 4 Islands tour

Use this section to decide what to book for Monday 9 November.

---

## Quick recommendation

### Choose Krabi 4 Islands if:

- You want a shorter, easier day.
- You want less time on a speedboat.
- You want to keep the anniversary day relaxed.
- You want classic Krabi beaches and limestone scenery.
- You want to reduce the risk of feeling rushed or exhausted.
- You prefer a tour closer to Ao Nang.
- You want more flexibility for your anniversary dinner.

### Choose Phi Phi Islands if:

- Phi Phi is a bucket-list destination for you.
- You want dramatic, iconic scenery and photos.
- You are happy to do a full-day tour.
- You do not mind an early start.
- You are comfortable with more crowds.
- You are okay with more time on a speedboat.
- You want to say you have been to Phi Phi during this trip.

### Be cautious with Phi Phi if:

- You dislike crowded places.
- You get seasick easily.
- You want a very relaxed anniversary day.
- You do not want a long, structured tour day.
- You are worried about returning in time for sunset dinner.
- You already have several other island days on the trip.

## What to expect: Phi Phi Islands tour

### Typical pickup

- Early morning pickup from Ao Nang, usually around 7:00–8:00am.
- Speedboat or large boat depending on operator and package.
- Small-group speedboat tours are usually better than large party boats.

### Typical stops

Depending on weather, tides, national park rules and operator route, the tour may include:

- Phi Phi Leh
- Maya Bay, if open and landing is allowed
- Pileh Lagoon
- Viking Cave, often viewed from the boat
- Monkey Beach, conditions permitting
- Snorkelling stops around limestone cliffs
- Lunch on Phi Phi Don or on the boat

### Important note about Maya Bay

Maya Bay can be subject to:

- Seasonal closure
- Conservation rules
- No-swimming rules
- No-landing rules
- Time limits
- Crowd management

Do not assume Maya Bay will allow swimming or beach access. Confirm with the operator before booking.

### What the day feels like

A Phi Phi tour is more of a full production day.

Expect:

- Early start
- More time on the boat
- More people
- More structured stops
- More walking and movement
- More sun exposure
- More chance of feeling tired by the evening

It can be amazing, but it is less relaxed than the Krabi 4 Islands tour.

### Best choice if you want:

- Famous photos
- Iconic limestone scenery
- A “big trip” feeling
- A memorable anniversary adventure day

### Worst choice if you want:

- A slow, low-effort anniversary day
- Minimal crowds
- Short boat rides
- Guaranteed sunset dinner timing

---

## What to expect: Krabi 4 Islands tour

### Typical pickup

Krabi 4 Islands tours are often offered as:

- Morning half-day
- Afternoon half-day
- Sunset-style tour, depending on operator and season

Pickup times vary, so confirm the exact schedule when booking.

### Typical stops

The exact route can vary, but common stops include:

- Phra Nang Cave Beach
- Chicken Island
- Koh Poda
- Koh Tup / Koh Mor, depending on tide and conditions

### What the day feels like

This is usually a more manageable island tour from Ao Nang.

Expect:

- Shorter boat rides
- Less travel time
- Easier return to Ao Nang
- Good swimming and snorkelling stops
- Classic limestone scenery
- Beautiful beaches
- Less exhaustion than Phi Phi

### Best choice if you want:

- A romantic but not exhausting anniversary day
- Classic Krabi island scenery
- Easier logistics
- More control over dinner timing
- A tour closer to Ao Nang

### Worst choice if you want:

- The most famous island destination in the region
- Phi Phi-style dramatic scenery
- A full-day adventure

## My suggested decision rule

If this is meant to feel romantic and not too tiring, I would choose Krabi 4 Islands.

If you choose Krabi 4 Islands, try to book a morning departure if available so you can still have a sunset anniversary dinner.

If Phi Phi is a major bucket-list goal and you do not mind a longer, busier day, choose Phi Phi.

If you choose Phi Phi, do not overplan the evening. Keep the anniversary dinner relaxed and reserve somewhere close to your hotel or beachfront.

## What to expect on the Hong Islands tour

The Hong Islands are one of the best easy island days from Ao Nang. They offer a good mix of:

- Limestone scenery
- Beach time
- Snorkelling
- Swimming

# Gluten-free game plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but gluten can enter through:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Sauces
- Battered foods
- Fried foods
- Shared fryers
- Cross-contamination

For a strict requirement, explain the situation clearly rather than simply saying “no bread.”

Useful Thai phrase:

ฉันแพ้กลูเตน
Chan pae gluten
“I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”

For a strict requirement:

อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน
“The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food containing gluten.”

For the anniversary dinner, contact the restaurant ahead of time and discuss the requirement before arrival.

For tours, mention the gluten-free requirement when booking, especially where lunch is included.
