## Decision guide: Phi Phi Islands tour vs Krabi 4 Islands tour

Use this section to decide what to book for Monday 9 November.

### Quick recommendation

Choose Krabi 4 Islands if:
You want a shorter, easier day.
You want less time on a speedboat.
You want to keep the tour day relaxed.
You want classic Krabi beaches and limestone scenery.
You want to reduce the risk of feeling rushed or exhausted.
You prefer a tour closer to Ao Nang.
You want more flexibility for your evening dinner.

Choose Phi Phi Islands if:
Phi Phi is a bucket-list destination for you.
You want dramatic, iconic scenery and photos.
You are happy to do a full-day tour.
You do not mind an early start.
You are comfortable with more crowds.
You are okay with more time on a speedboat.
You want to say you have been to Phi Phi during this trip.

Be cautious with Phi Phi if:
You dislike crowded places.
You get seasick easily.
You want a very relaxed tour day.
You do not want a long, structured tour day.
You are worried about returning in time for a relaxed dinner.
You already have several other island days on the trip.

### What to expect: Phi Phi Islands tour

Typical pickup
Early morning pickup from Ao Nang, usually around 7:00–8:00am. Small-group speedboat tours are usually better than large party boats.

Typical stops
Phi Phi Leh, Maya Bay (if open), Pileh Lagoon, Viking Cave, Monkey Beach, Snorkelling stops, Lunch on Phi Phi Don or on the boat.

Important note about Maya Bay
Maya Bay can be subject to seasonal closure, conservation rules, no-swimming/no-landing rules, and crowd management. Do not assume Maya Bay will allow swimming or beach access. Confirm with the operator before booking.

What the day feels like
A full production day. Early start, more time on the boat, more people, more structured stops, more sun exposure. It can be amazing, but it is less relaxed than the Krabi 4 Islands tour.

Best choice if you want:
Famous photos, Iconic limestone scenery, A “big trip” feeling, A memorable adventure day.

Worst choice if you want:
A slow, low-effort tour day, Minimal crowds, Short boat rides, Guaranteed early dinner timing.

### What to expect: Krabi 4 Islands tour

Typical pickup
Often offered as morning half-day, afternoon half-day, or sunset-style tour.

Typical stops
Phra Nang Cave Beach, Chicken Island, Koh Poda, Koh Tup / Koh Mor.

What the day feels like
More manageable. Shorter boat rides, less travel time, easier return, good swimming, classic limestone scenery, less exhaustion.

Best choice if you want:
A fun but not exhausting tour day, Classic Krabi island scenery, Easier logistics, More control over dinner timing.

Worst choice if you want:
The most famous island destination in the region, Phi Phi-style dramatic scenery, A full-day adventure.

### How each option affects your evening dinner

| Tour choice                              | Likely return time | Best evening dinner plan                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Krabi 4 Islands, morning tour            | Early afternoon    | Sunset drinks + 7:30pm Relaxed Thai dinner  |
| Krabi 4 Islands, afternoon / sunset tour | Around 6:30–7:30pm | Late relaxed Thai dinner around 8:00pm      |
| Phi Phi Islands full-day tour            | Around 4:30–5:30pm | Early relaxed dinner or 8:00pm if exhausted |

### My suggested decision rule

If you want the tour day to feel fun but not too tiring, I would choose Krabi 4 Islands.
If you choose Krabi 4 Islands, try to book a morning departure if available so you have plenty of time to rest before dinner.
If Phi Phi is a major bucket-list goal and you do not mind a longer, busier day, choose Phi Phi. Keep the evening dinner relaxed and reserve somewhere close to your hotel or beachfront.

## Gluten-free game plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but watch out for soy sauce, marinades, battered foods, and shared fryers.

Useful Thai phrase: ฉันแพ้กลูเตน (Chan pae gluten) - “I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”
Strict requirement: อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน

Contact restaurants ahead of time, especially for your Day 1 Anniversary Dinner.

## What I would skip or be careful with

Do not automatically add Phi Phi unless it's a bucket-list priority (adds fatigue and crowds).
Do not add both Phi Phi and Krabi 4 Islands (too many boat days).
Skip uncertain direct Railay → Koh Lanta boat transfers (Use the Ao Nang land route instead).
Skip extreme activities (Rock climbing, scuba diving, Railay lagoon scramble).

## Important logistical notes

Singapore overnight: Stay at Carlton City Hotel Singapore, keep the evening simple with dinner at Maxwell Food Centre, and leave for Changi Airport in enough time for your Sunday morning flight.

Railay → Koh Lanta transfer: Railay → Ao Nang by longtail boat → Grab / taxi / private transfer to Koh Lanta. Keep luggage manageable and wear sandals for the beach landing.

Grab availability: Have a backup plan or pre-book a private car for the Ao Nang to Koh Lanta transfer.

Early November schedules: Confirm boat and tour schedules upon arrival as early Nov is close to the start of high season.

Anniversary dinner timing: Your anniversary dinner is now scheduled for your arrival night (Sunday 8 Nov). Book your anniversary restaurant in Ao Nang for around 7:30pm on Day 1. Keep Day 2 (your island tour day) evening completely relaxed with a casual Thai dinner like Jungle Kitchen, as you will likely be tired from the sun and boat.
