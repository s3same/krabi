# 8-Day Krabi Itinerary - Proposed Revised Itinerary (1 Night Railay / 3 Nights Lanta)

**Dates:** 8–15 November 2026
**Travellers:** Couple, age 24
**Style:** Relaxing, culturally immersive, trendy, adventurous
**Dietary requirement:** One traveller is gluten-free
**Arrival:** Krabi Airport, 8 Nov at 11:40am
**Departure:** Krabi Airport, 15 Nov at 5:30pm
**Special occasion:** 6-month anniversary dinner on 9 Nov

---

## Recommended route

**Ao Nang → Railay → Koh Lanta**

This revised version keeps the same three distinct experiences, but changes the balance:

- **Ao Nang:** convenience, cafés, nightlife, Krabi Town, Hong Islands, anniversary dinner
- **Railay:** dramatic limestone scenery, beaches and a short romantic stay
- **Koh Lanta:** slow island life, culture, beaches, snorkelling and the final wind-down

The main change is reducing Railay from **2 nights to 1 night** and giving the extra night to **Koh Lanta**.

This is a better fit if you are worried that Railay may not have enough “things to do” without rock climbing, scuba diving or the slippery Railay Lagoon scramble. Railay is beautiful, but it is small, quiet at night and more limited for dining than Ao Nang or Koh Lanta.

This version gives you one sunset/evening in Railay, then moves you to Koh Lanta for a longer, more relaxed finish.

---

## What changed from the previous version

### Railay: reduced from 2 nights to 1 night

Railay is scenic and romantic, but for your travel style, one night is likely enough.

You will still get:

- Railay West sunset
- Dramatic limestone scenery
- Beachfront paths
- A romantic, quieter evening
- The feeling of staying somewhere special without the day-trip crowds

But you avoid:

- A second evening with limited dining and nightlife
- Feeling like you have run out of things to do
- Staying somewhere too quiet for your preferences if you prefer cafés, restaurants and culture

### Koh Lanta: increased from 2 nights to 3 nights

The extra night goes to Koh Lanta because it better matches your overall style:

- More relaxing
- More culturally immersive
- Better for food
- Better for slow island life
- Better for sunsets, beach time and a calm end to the trip

Koh Lanta is also the best place to gain an extra night because it gives you time for a proper cultural day without rushing.

### Phi Phi: still skipped

Do not add Phi Phi back in.

Phi Phi would add:

- More ferry time
- More crowds
- More logistics
- Less relaxation
- Overlapping island scenery when you already have Hong Islands, Railay and Koh Lanta

Your trip already has the right mix of limestone scenery, snorkelling, beaches and culture without adding Phi Phi.

## Day 1 - Sunday 8 Nov

### Arrival → Ao Nang → sunset

#### 11:40am - Arrive at Krabi Airport

Take a pre-booked transfer or taxi directly to Ao Nang.

#### 1:00pm - Arrive and drop bags

Check into your Ao Nang hotel.

#### 2:00pm - Lunch + café

Try **Shatterbox Kitchen & Bar** or another modern Ao Nang café.

#### 4:30pm - Ao Nang Beach

Take a relaxed beachfront walk.

#### 6:00pm - Sunset drinks

Find a beachfront bar or café and ease into the holiday.

#### 7:30pm - Thai dinner

Try **Jungle Kitchen** for a more local-feeling meal rather than eating directly on the busiest tourist strip.

#### Stay

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**
Alternative: **COSI Krabi Ao Nang Beach**

---

## Day 2 - Monday 9 Nov

### 💕 6-month anniversary

### Krabi Town + anniversary sunset dinner

Keep the daytime local and relaxed, then return to Ao Nang early enough to dress up and enjoy a proper sunset dinner for the anniversary.

#### 9:00am - Slow breakfast in Ao Nang

Have breakfast at your hotel or a nearby café, then head toward Krabi Town.

#### 10:30am - Krabi Town

Explore the town centre at a relaxed pace and take in the riverside, local shops and everyday southern-Thai atmosphere.

#### Lunch

Choose a local restaurant or café in town.

With the gluten-free requirement, confirm sauces, marinades and preparation methods before ordering.

#### Afternoon - Riverside + local sights

Continue exploring Krabi Town, but keep the afternoon flexible rather than staying too late.

The priority is getting back to Ao Nang with time to shower, dress up and reach the coast before golden hour.

#### 3:30–4:00pm - Return to Ao Nang

Head back to the hotel, freshen up and get ready for the anniversary dinner.

#### 5:00–5:30pm - Arrive for sunset

Aim to be seated before sunset.

Current dining guidance for Ao Nang puts sunset around **6:15–6:45pm**, with beachfront sunset tables often worth reserving ahead.

#### 5:30–7:30pm - Anniversary dinner

Your strongest options are:

##### REEVE Beach Club Krabi

A polished beachfront option on Ao Nang Beach with all-day dining, cocktails, live music and daily fire-dance entertainment.

Best for: a lively, stylish beach-club atmosphere with dinner, drinks and entertainment.

Plan: reserve a table close to the beachfront and ask for a sunset-facing position.

##### Katara Bar & Restaurant / Katara Beach Club Krabi

A more design-led beachfront dining experience with its “Beach Theater” concept, modern Thai flavours, drinks and broad Andaman views.

Best for: a more contemporary, couple-friendly setting where the view is the main event.

Plan: reserve in advance and request one of the seats or tables with the clearest horizon view.

##### Other sunset / beach-club-style options

- **The Beach Seafood & Grill** — Directly on the sands of Ao Nang Beach. Good for a classic beach dinner and sunset.
- **The Last Fisherman Bar** — Casual, toes-in-the-sand beach-bar option. Better for a relaxed, barefoot evening.
- **C2 Beach Club** — Quieter beachfront bar toward the southern end of Ao Nang. Better for casual drinks than a formal anniversary dinner.
- **Lae Lay Grill** — Hillside seafood restaurant with elevated views. Best if you want a wide panorama rather than being on the sand.

#### My anniversary setup

Build the evening around:

- 5:00–5:30pm arrival
- Sunset around 6:15–6:45pm
- Dinner through 7:30pm
- A relaxed drink or short beach walk before returning to the hotel

#### Gluten-free requirement

Contact the restaurant before booking.

Use clear wording:

> “One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be avoided.”

For a strict requirement, confirm sauces, marinades, batter, frying oil and shared preparation surfaces before ordering.

#### 8:00pm onward - Easy anniversary night

Return to the hotel without adding another major activity. The next morning is your early Hong Islands day.

#### Stay

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**
Alternative: **COSI Krabi Ao Nang Beach**

## Day 3 - Tuesday 10 Nov

### Ao Nang 🐠 Hong Islands + snorkelling

This is your first major tropical day.

#### 7:00–7:30am - Depart Ao Nang

Book a small-group Hong Islands tour rather than a large party boat.

Look for an itinerary including:

- Hong Island lagoon
- Snorkelling
- Swimming
- Lading/Pakbia area
- Beach time
- Lunch

#### What to request

Small group, early departure, snorkelling, Hong Lagoon, no party boat.

#### 2:30–3:00pm - Return to Ao Nang

Shower, nap and relax.

#### 4:30pm - Sunset

If you have the energy, head to **Khaothong Hill** for elevated views over the limestone islands.

Otherwise, keep it easy at Ao Nang.

#### Dinner

Try a contemporary restaurant such as **MESA Restaurant & Bar**, or choose another gluten-free-friendly option after checking with the restaurant.

#### Stay

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**
Alternative: **COSI Krabi Ao Nang Beach**

## Day 4 - Wednesday 11 Nov

### Ao Thalane kayaking → Railay

This is your adventure-without-being-extreme day, followed by your first taste of Railay.

Because you are only staying one night in Railay, keep your luggage practical. Railay is accessed by boat and can involve sand/beach landings, so avoid overpacking.

#### 7:30am - Ao Thalane kayaking

Book a guided kayaking experience.

Expect:

- Mangrove channels
- Limestone cliffs
- Jungle
- Wildlife
- Calm waterways

#### 1:00pm - Lunch + collect luggage

Return toward Ao Nang, have lunch and collect your bags.

#### 2:00pm-ish - Transfer to Railay

Use the simplest confirmed boat/longtail connection available from Ao Nang to Railay.

Because boat services can be seasonal, confirm the exact 11 November service before relying on it.

#### Late afternoon - Check in + Railay West

Check into your Railay hotel, drop your bags and head straight out for a gentle first walk.

Prioritise:

- Railay West
- Beachfront paths
- Limestone scenery
- Sunset photos

Do not try to rush through every Railay sight on this first afternoon.

#### Sunset + dinner

Stay around Railay West for sunset, then have an easy dinner close to the hotel.

This first Railay night should feel like a change of pace after the kayaking rather than another packed itinerary day.

#### Suggested Railay dinner options

##### Sunset / beachfront

- **Krua Village Restaurant at Railay Village Resort**
- **The Sunset Restaurant at Sand Sea Resort**

##### Thai / local

- **Railay Family Restaurant**
- **Mangrove Restaurant**
- **Welcome Back Restaurant**

##### Indian / gluten-free options

- **Kohinoor Indian Restaurant & Pizza**

Even where gluten-free options are listed, still confirm preparation practices before ordering.

#### Stay

**Railay hotel/resort — 1 night**

Choose a Railay hotel/resort that prioritises:

- Location
- Atmosphere
- Beach access
- Walkability to Railay West and restaurants

## Day 5 - Thursday 12 Nov

### Railay morning → Koh Lanta transfer + beach sunset

Today is the main logistical trade-off of the 1-night Railay plan.

You will not have a full leisurely Railay day. Instead, you get one evening and one morning, then move on to Koh Lanta for the slower, longer final part of the trip.

#### 7:30am - Breakfast

Have a relaxed breakfast.

#### 8:00–9:45am - Short Railay walk or final photos

Use this time for the highlights you most want to see.

Good options:

- Walk Railay West
- Take morning beach photos
- Visit Phra Nang Beach if you have time
- See Princess Cave if it is convenient
- Enjoy the limestone scenery without rushing

Skip the Railay viewpoint/lagoon scramble because it is a slippery, rope-assisted climb and does not fit your preference for avoiding extreme activities.

#### 10:00am - Check out

Pack up and leave some buffer for the island transfer.

#### Late morning / early afternoon - Railay → Koh Lanta

Use a boat only if the exact seasonal service is confirmed for 12 November.

Otherwise, use a minivan + longtail/ferry combination as the reliable default.

The key objective is to arrive in Koh Lanta with enough afternoon left to settle in.

Important: this transfer can take several hours. Do not plan another major activity today.

#### 3:00–4:00pm - Check in

Stay around **Long Beach** or **Klong Dao** for easy access to the beach, restaurants and sunset.

#### 4:30pm - Slow beach afternoon

Pool, beach or a short walk.

Do not schedule another major excursion.

#### 5:30pm - Beach sunset

This is your first Koh Lanta sunset.

#### 7:30pm - Dinner

Keep the evening relaxed.

Good options:

- **Cozy Restaurant** — good gluten-free-friendly option, but still confirm preparation
- **Rareview** — waterfront / photos
- **Yang Garden Restaurant** — stylish / fusion
- **Nomad Beach Bar Koh Lanta** — beachy / trendy

#### Stay

**Long Beach Chalet — Night 1 of 3**
Alternative: **Coco Lanta Resort**

## Day 6 - Friday 13 Nov

### Koh Lanta culture, Old Town + sunset day

This is the day that benefits most from the extra night. Instead of rushing Koh Lanta, you now have time to enjoy the island’s culture, scenery and food at a relaxed pace.

#### 8:30am - Breakfast

Have a slow breakfast at the resort.

#### 10:00am - Lanta Old Town

Head to **Lanta Old Town** by scooter, taxi or private transfer.

If you are comfortable on a scooter, this can be a fun way to see the island. If not, use a taxi or private car for a stress-free cultural half-day.

Photo and culture highlights:

- Wooden shophouses
- Waterfront stilt houses
- Old pier
- Chinese shrine
- Riverside-style Old Town streets
- Local cafés and small shops

#### Lunch

Have a waterfront lunch.

Good option:

- **Panorama Oceanfront Restaurant**

Alternatively, choose a casual local restaurant in or near Lanta Old Town.

For gluten-free needs, confirm sauces, marinades, frying oil and cross-contamination before ordering.

#### Afternoon - Flexible island time

Choose one depending on energy levels:

##### Relaxed option

Return to the hotel for pool time, beach time and a slow afternoon.

##### Light adventure option

Visit a quieter scenic spot such as:

- Tung Yee Peng mangrove area
- Kantiang Bay viewpoint
- Southern Lanta coastal roads

Keep it light. This is not meant to be another exhausting tour day.

#### 5:30pm - Sunset

Good sunset options:

- Long Beach
- Klong Dao Beach
- Kantiang Bay if you are out exploring the south

#### 7:30pm - Dinner

Make this a nice but relaxed evening.

Suggested options:

- **Yang Garden Restaurant** — stylish / fusion
- **Nomad Beach Bar Koh Lanta** — beachy / trendy
- **Cozy Restaurant** — practical gluten-free-friendly option

#### Stay

**Long Beach Chalet — Night 2 of 3**
Alternative: **Coco Lanta Resort**

## Day 7 - Saturday 14 Nov

### Koh Lanta 4 Islands + Emerald Cave

Make this the final major adventure day, then return to the resort for an easy last evening.

#### Early morning - Depart Koh Lanta

Book a Koh Lanta 4 Islands / Emerald Cave tour.

Potential stops include:

- Koh Mook
- Koh Kradan
- Koh Ngai
- Koh Cheuak
- Emerald Cave

#### Activities

- Snorkelling
- Swimming
- Beaches
- Boat rides
- Emerald Cave exploration

Bring a waterproof phone pouch for the Emerald Cave swim.

#### 5:00pm - Return to resort

Do nothing.

Shower, nap, sunset and enjoy your final dinner on the island.

#### Gluten-free

#### Dinner

Try a gluten-free-friendly option such as **Cozy Restaurant**, after confirming preparation practices.

Alternatively, choose somewhere with a relaxed atmosphere for your final island dinner.

#### Stay

**Long Beach Chalet — Night 3 of 3**
Alternative: **Coco Lanta Resort**

## Day 8 - Sunday 15 Nov

### Koh Lanta morning → Krabi Airport

Finishing in Koh Lanta means the last day is deliberately simple: no final hotel change and no need to rush through another sightseeing block.

Your flight is not until 5:30pm, so use the morning as the wind-down.

#### 8:00am - Breakfast

Have a slow breakfast at the resort.

#### 9:00–11:00am - Beach / pool / final photos

Choose whichever feels nicest:

- Final swim
- Pool time
- Beach walk
- Last photos
- Coffee or fruit shake

#### 11:00am - Check out

Have an early lunch before leaving the island.

#### Around 12:00–12:30pm - Leave Koh Lanta

Take a pre-booked private or shared minivan transfer toward Krabi Airport.

Build in a generous road-transfer buffer rather than trying to make the afternoon tight.

#### 3:00–3:15pm - Aim to arrive at airport

This gives you a comfortable buffer before your 5:30pm flight.

## 🌾 Gluten-free game plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but gluten can enter through:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Sauces
- Battered foods
- Fried foods
- Shared fryers
- Cross-contamination

For a strict requirement, explain the situation clearly rather than simply saying “no bread.”

Useful Thai phrase:

**ฉันแพ้กลูเตน**
**Chan pae gluten**
“I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”

For a strict requirement:

For tours, mention the gluten-free requirement when booking, especially where lunch is included.

## Important logistical note

The main caveat of this revised itinerary is the **Railay → Koh Lanta transfer on 12 November**.

Because Railay is boat-access only and Koh Lanta requires a longer transfer, this day can take a large part of the daytime.

To make it work:

- Confirm the transfer before arrival
- Keep luggage manageable
- Check out of Railay by 10:00am
- Do not plan a major activity on this day
- Expect it to be a travel day, not a sightseeing day
- Use a minivan-based transfer if boat/ferry schedules are uncertain

If you strongly dislike travel days or want to avoid the risk of a complicated transfer, the alternative is to skip staying in Railay altogether, stay 4 nights in Ao Nang, and visit Railay as a half-day longtail boat trip before continuing to Koh Lanta.

But if you still want the experience of waking up in Railay once, this 1-night Railay / 3-night Koh Lanta version is the strongest revised balance.
