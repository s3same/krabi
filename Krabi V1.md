# 8-Day Krabi Itinerary - 8--15 November 2026

## Trip profile

- **Travellers:** Couple, age 24
- **Style:** Relaxing, culturally immersive, trendy, adventurous
- **Activities:** Island hopping, snorkelling, kayaking, beaches,
  food, cafés, markets
- **Avoid:** Extreme activities such as rock climbing and scuba diving
- **Dietary requirement:** One traveller is gluten-free
- **Arrival:** Krabi Airport, 8 November at 11:40am
- **Departure:** Krabi Airport, 15 November at 5:30pm
- **Special occasion:** 6-month anniversary on 14 November

## Recommended route

**Krabi Town → Ao Nang/Railay → Koh Lanta → Ao Nang**

This keeps the trip varied without requiring constant hotel changes.

# Day 1 - Sunday 8 Nov

## Arrive → Krabi Town → local food + Walking Street

### 11:40am - Arrive at Krabi Airport

Take a pre-booked transfer/taxi into Krabi Town rather than immediately
heading to Ao Nang. This gives you an easy first afternoon and a more
local introduction to Krabi.

### 1:00pm - Check in + lunch

Stay around central Krabi Town so you can walk around easily.

For coffee and photos, try **Much & Mellow Cafe Krabi Town**.

### Afternoon - Explore Krabi Town

Walk through:

- Wat Kaew Korawaram
- Maharaj Road
- Black Crab Plaza
- Khao Khanab Nam riverside area

The Black Crab sculpture and limestone peaks make a particularly good
first-day photo stop.

### 6:00pm onwards - Maharaj Walking Street

Since this is Sunday, spend the evening at the Walking Street market.

Treat it as your first Thai food crawl: grilled seafood, rice dishes,
fruit, desserts and snacks.

### Photo hits

- Black Crab Plaza
- Old-town streets
- Temple details
- Market lights

### Gluten-free strategy

Prioritize rice-based dishes, grilled seafood/meat, curries and fresh
fruit. Be especially careful with:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Wheat-containing sauces
- Fried foods
- Shared fryers

### Stay

**Play Poshtel Krabi** - youthful, central and budget-friendly.

Alternative: **ROYAL PINEAPPLE HOTEL**.

# Day 2 - Monday 9 Nov

## Krabi Town → Ao Nang → Railay

### 9:00am - Transfer to Ao Nang

Check into your Ao Nang hotel.

### Brunch

Try **TAN Tribe Cafe, Aonang Beach** for a modern café experience.

### 11:30am - Longtail boat to Railay

Take a shared longtail boat from Ao Nang to Railay.

### Afternoon - Railay

Visit:

- Railay West
- Phra Nang Beach
- Princess Cave
- Beachfront areas

Skip the Railay viewpoint/lagoon climb because it is a slippery,
rope-assisted scramble and doesn't fit your preference for avoiding
extreme activities.

### 4:30pm - Golden hour at Railay West

Stay for the sunset light, then return to Ao Nang before regular
longtail service winds down.

### Dinner

For inexpensive, authentic Thai food:

- **Kodam Kitchen Branch 1 Aonang**
- **Ton Ma Yom Thaifood, Aonang**

### Stay

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**

Alternative: **COSI Krabi Ao Nang Beach**

# Day 3 - Tuesday 10 Nov

## Hong Islands - tropical island day

This should be one of the highlights of the trip.

### 7:00--7:30am - Depart Ao Nang

Book a **small-group Hong Islands tour**, rather than a large party
boat.

Look for an itinerary including:

- Hong Island lagoon
- Snorkelling
- Swimming
- Lading/Pakbia area
- Beach time
- Lunch

### What to ask the tour operator for

> Small group, early departure, snorkelling, Hong Lagoon, no party boat.

This should give you a more relaxed experience.

### 2:30--3:00pm - Return to Ao Nang

Shower, nap and relax.

### 4:30pm - Khaothong Hill

Head to **Khaothong Hill** for sunset.

This is one of the best combinations of:

- Ocean views
- Limestone islands
- Café/restaurant atmosphere
- Couple photos
- Sunset

### Dinner

Have a light dinner/snacks at Khaothong Hill or return to Ao Nang.

# Day 4 - Wednesday 11 Nov

## Ao Thalane kayaking → Koh Lanta

This is your adventure day without anything extreme.

### 7:30am - Ao Thalane kayaking

Book a guided kayaking experience through **Ao Thalane**.

Expect:

- Mangrove channels
- Limestone cliffs
- Jungle
- Wildlife
- Calm waterways

This is much more relaxed than open-ocean or adrenaline-heavy
activities.

### 1:00pm - Lunch + collect luggage

### 2:00--3:00pm - Transfer to Koh Lanta

Use a shared/private minivan rather than depending on a seasonal ferry
schedule.

### 5:00pm - Check in + beach sunset

Stay around **Long Beach or Klong Dao** on Koh Lanta's west coast.

Have a relaxed beachfront dinner.

### Stay

**Long Beach Chalet**

Alternative: **Coco Lanta Resort**

# Day 5 - Thursday 12 Nov

Book a **Koh Lanta 4 Islands / Emerald Cave** tour.

Typical stops may include:

- Emerald Cave

Expect:

- Snorkelling

- Boat rides

It's adventurous without requiring:

- Diving
- Rock climbing

### Around 5:00pm - Return to the resort

Keep the evening completely free.

Shower, nap, sunset and dinner.

### Dinner

Restaurant** is one option to investigate.

# Day 6 - Friday 13 Nov

## Koh Lanta cultural day

Make this your slowest day.

### Morning - Sleep in

Have breakfast, swim and relax.

### 11:30am - Lanta Old Town

Explore the traditional wooden shophouses and waterfront.

Lanta Old Town is especially worthwhile for cultural immersion because
of Koh Lanta's mix of Thai-Chinese, Thai-Muslim and Sea Gypsy heritage.

### Lunch / coffee

Try a waterfront café.

**Rareview** is a good option for an over-water setting and photos.

### Afternoon - Tung Yee Peng

Visit **Tung Yee Peng** and consider a community-based mangrove
experience.

This gives you a more local perspective through:

- Mangroves
- Community tourism
- Local livelihoods
- Conservation
- Longtail boats

### Late afternoon - Kantiang Bay

Finish the day with beach time and sunset.

### Dinner

Try **Yang Garden Restaurant** for a more stylish/fusion dinner.

table.

Saffron is an elegant Thai restaurant at Banyan Tree Krabi and is the
strongest fit for your special-occasion dinner.

### Important gluten-free note

Contact the restaurant in advance and clearly explain:

> "One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal due to dietary
> requirements."

The restaurant's current menu identifies gluten-free dishes and asks
guests to communicate food allergies/special dietary requirements.

Examples of dishes currently marked gluten-free include:

- Pomelo salad with prawns
- Turmeric chicken soup
- Tom kha gai
- Tom yum goong
- Green curry

### Anniversary photo idea

Arrive early enough for golden-hour photos and ask for a table with a

Dress up - this is the night to go full vacation-couple mode.

# 🌾 Gluten-free game plan

Thai food contains many naturally gluten-free ingredients, but gluten
can enter dishes through:

- Soy sauce
- Marinades
- Sauces
- Battered foods
- Fried foods
- Shared cooking surfaces
- Cross-contamination

For a strict requirement, explain the situation clearly rather than
simply saying "no bread."

Useful Thai phrase:

> **ฉันแพ้กลูเตน**\
> _Chan pae gluten_\
> "I am allergic/intolerant to gluten."

For a strict requirement:

> **อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน**\
> "The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food
> containing gluten."

For the anniversary dinner at Saffron, contact the restaurant ahead of
time and discuss the dietary requirement before arriving.

# Things I would NOT add

## Phi Phi

I would skip Phi Phi on this trip.

It's beautiful, but your itinerary already has:

- Hong Islands
- Railay
- Koh Lanta
- Emerald Cave
- Snorkelling
- Kayaking

Adding Phi Phi would introduce another busy island day and more
transport, which works against your goal of having a relaxing holiday.

## Extreme activities

I would also skip:

- Rock climbing
- Scuba diving
- Railay lagoon scramble
- Other technical/adrenaline-heavy activities

Your trip is better suited to:

- Snorkelling
- Kayaking
- Swimming
- Longtail boats
- Beaches
- Food
- Cafés
- Markets
- Cultural experiences
