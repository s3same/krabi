### Tour highlights

- Snorkel among colorful marine life and vibrant coral reefs at **Chicken Island**
- Walk along the unique sandbar at **Koh Tup** and take photos

### Full description / booking notes

The supplied tour description is a premium longtail-boat experience visiting four famous Krabi islands, with a shared or private option, lunch, fresh fruit, water and snorkelling equipment.

Typical sequence:

1. **Chicken Island** – snorkelling around the island's distinctive rock formation and coral reef.
2. **Koh Tup / Thale Waek** – sandbar experience connecting Koh Tup and Koh Mor during low tide.
3. **Koh Poda** – lunch on the beach, swimming, sunbathing and snorkelling.
4. **Phra Nang Cave Beach** – limestone cliffs, clear water and the famous cave in the Railay area.

Private tours may offer early-morning or sunset options depending on the operator/package.

### Lunch

**Included with the selected tour/package.** Confirm the gluten-free meal before booking.

### Hotel

**Kokotel Krabi Ao Nang**

### Gluten-free note

Tell the tour operator about the gluten-free requirement when booking, especially because lunch is included.

Suggested wording:

> “One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely and whether cross-contamination can be avoided.”

For dinner:

> “One guest requires a strict gluten-free meal. Please advise which dishes can be prepared safely or if you have dedicated fryers/surfaces.”

Useful Thai phrases:

- **ฉันแพ้กลูเตน (Chan pae gluten)** – “I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”
- **อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน** – “The food must be gluten-free and prepared separately from food containing gluten.”

# Gluten-Free Game Plan

Thai cuisine has many naturally gluten-free dishes, but watch out for **soy sauce, marinades, battered foods and shared fryers**.

## Useful Thai phrase

**ฉันแพ้กลูเตน (Chan pae gluten)** – “I am allergic/intolerant to gluten.”

## Strict requirement

**อาหารต้องปลอดกลูเตน และแยกการปรุงจากอาหารที่มีกลูเตน**

## Booking rule for tours

Tell every operator about the gluten-free requirement **before booking**, especially where lunch is included.

## Restaurant rule

Contact restaurants ahead of time, especially for the **Day 1 anniversary dinner**.

# Important Logistical Notes

- **Singapore overnight:** Stay at Carlton City Hotel Singapore, keep the evening simple with dinner at Maxwell Food Centre, and leave for Changi Airport in enough time for the Sunday morning flight.
- **Day 2 booking:** Compare the three supplied Krabi 4 Islands booking links immediately before paying and confirm the **9 Nov 2026** date, pickup point, lunch inclusion and gluten-free meal handling.
- **Day 3 booking:** Confirm the **10 Nov 2026** date, Ao Nang pickup, optional kayak cost, 360° viewpoint option and gluten-free lunch handling.

- **Railay → Koh Lanta:** Railay → Ao Nang by longtail boat → Grab/taxi/private transfer to Koh Lanta. Keep luggage manageable and wear sandals for the beach landing.
- **Grab availability:** Have a backup plan or pre-book a private car for the Ao Nang to Koh Lanta transfer.
- **Early November schedules:** Confirm boat and tour schedules upon arrival as early Nov is close to the start of high season.
- **Anniversary dinner:** Book the anniversary restaurant in Ao Nang for around **7:30pm on Day 1**. Keep Day 2 evening completely relaxed with a casual Thai dinner because the day will be sun- and boat-heavy.
